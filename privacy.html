Privacy Policy

Transparency Scorecard · Last updated July 23, 2026

This page explains what the Transparency Scorecard does with the email address you enter at the gateway. We wrote it to match how the tool actually behaves — nothing more.

Who we are

The data controller is Meek Earth Studio PBC, a Washington, D.C.–area public benefit corporation. For any question about this notice or your data, contact cbreid3@meekearthstudio.net.

What we collect

When you enter your email address to open the experience, we collect:

  • Your email address;
  • The date and time you submitted it; and
  • A source label identifying that it came from this scorecard.

We do not ask for or collect anything else — no name, no organization, no payment details.

What we don't collect

Your scorecard answers and your score are calculated entirely in your browser and are never transmitted to us. Only your email address is sent when you submit the gateway form.

Why we collect it

To send you your scorecard results and occasional updates from Meek Earth Studio about our work and future benefit events.

Where it goes

On submission, your email is sent to our own endpoint on this site (/api/capture), which records it in our contact list — a Google Sheet and/or HubSpot. Google and HubSpot process the data on our behalf as our service providers; we do not grant them the right to use your email for their own purposes.

What we never do

  • We don't sell your email address.
  • We don't share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Legal basis

We process your email address on the basis of your consent, given when you submit it at the gateway. You can withdraw that consent at any time (see "Your rights").

How long we keep it

We keep your email address until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it, whichever comes first. If you unsubscribe, we remove you from active mailings and retain only what's needed to honor that request.

Your rights

You can, at any time, ask us to:

  • Access the data we hold about you;
  • Correct it if it's wrong (rectification);
  • Delete it (erasure), or receive a copy to take elsewhere (portability); and
  • Unsubscribe / withdraw consent, which stops all future mailings.

To exercise any of these, email cbreid3@meekearthstudio.net and we'll take care of it. If you're in the EU or UK and believe we've mishandled your data, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

Changes

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we'll update this page and revise the date above.
